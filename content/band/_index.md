<h2>
L'osare è l'inizio del cambiamento
</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dario Crancy</strong>: vocals, guitar <small>(founder)</small>.</li>
<li><strong>Toma</strong>: guitar, backing vocals <small>(from 2012)</small>.</li>
<li><strong>Gio</strong>: bass, backing vocals <small>(from 2014)</small>.</li>
<li><strong>Pongo</strong>: drums, backing vocals <small>(founder)</small>.</li> 
</ul>
Previous members
<ul>
<li><small>Facche: vocals (2011-2022)</small> <strong>|</strong> <small>Kotikki: bass, backing vocals (1997-2013)</small> <strong>|</strong> <small>Ruggio: guitar, backing vocals (2006-2012)</small> <strong>|</strong> <small>Titti Cap&egrave;la: guitar, vocals (1997-2006).</small></li> 
</ul>
<br />
<p>
Dal 1997 portano sul palco tutta l'energia che li contraddistingue con un repertorio di brani originali ad alto impatto in stile punk rock con riff potenti e testi in italiano. Raccontano di esperienze vissute e riflessioni sul mondo che li circonda, sulle battaglie quotidiane vinte e perse, e sui momenti di sano e insano divertimento.
<br /> <br />
Nel roster dell'etichetta indipendente <strong>GASTERECORDS</strong> promuovono la propria musica e quella di altre band emergenti condividendo il palco con Cattive Abitudini, Vodka Juniors, L'invasione degli Omini Verdi, Los Fastidios, Totale Apatia, Bad Frog, Cornoltis, Jerry Moovers, Antefatti, Quarantena e aprendo i concerti di Punkreas, Marky Ramone's Blitzkrieg, ecc.<br /> <br />
</p>

 <img src="/images/crancy_crock_band.jpg" alt="Crancy Crock Band" width="800">
</div>
<h3>
 * Daring is the beginning of change
</h3>
<p>
<small> * Since 1997, they've brought their signature energy to the stage with a repertoire of high-impact, original punk rock songs with powerful riffs and lyrics in Italian. They share their experiences and reflections on the world around them, on daily battles won and lost, and on moments of wholesome and wild fun.</i>
<br /> <br />
 * On the roster of the independent label <strong>GASTERECORDS</strong> they promote their own music and that of other emerging bands, sharing the stage with Cattive Abitudini, Vodka Juniors, L'invasione degli Omini Verdi, Los Fastidios, Totale Apatia, Bad Frog, Cornoltis, Jerry Moovers, Antefatti, Quarantena and opening concerts for Punkreas, Marky Ramone's Blitzkrieg, etc. </small>
</p>
